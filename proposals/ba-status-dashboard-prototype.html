Project Canvas — the product at a glance
Customers
Clients booking appointments; Specialists offering services; Clinic admin overseeing operations.
Roles
Client · Specialist · Clinic Admin · System. — the acting roles the project's role model defines.
Problems
Phone booking is slow and error-prone; missed reminders cause no-shows; specialists juggle availability by hand.
Product
Thename
Acme Booking
Istype
An online appointment-booking platform.
Thatenables
Lets clients book specialists' published slots online and get reminders, instead of calling.
Forms
Responsive web app at MVP; native mobile a later phase.
Core Functions
Browse · book · cancel · publish availability · reminders · accounts & sign-in · admin oversight & exports · online payment (Phase 2).
Third-Party
Specialist calendar sync; email / SMS for reminders; payment provider (Phase 2).
Localization
English (default); single region & currency at MVP.
Competition
Unlike
Generic calendar-link tools and phone booking.
Our Solution
Specialist-published slots + a clinic role model + reminders tuned to the booking lifecycle.
Objectives
Cut no-shows; reduce phone-booking load; MVP live within the presale window. Measurable targets — to confirm.
