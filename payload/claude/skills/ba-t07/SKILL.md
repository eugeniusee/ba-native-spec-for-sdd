# `/ba-t07` — competitive analysis

**Serves:** Context **and** Vision — the catalogue's one dual-aspect technique.
**Class:** Context · **Destination:** `.specify/memory/competitive-analysis.md`,
plus the canvas `Competition.Unlike` summary by proposed-edit batch.

The alternatives the product is unlike — direct competitors, adjacent tools, and
**the status quo** — each carrying the differentiation-relevant deltas, keyed to
canvas problems and objectives, so the vision statement differentiates **against
named targets instead of a vague field**. The decision this run lets the BA make:
which alternatives matter at this grade, which deltas are real enough to
differentiate on — or that no market exists.

**This run supplies targets and deltas. It never writes the differentiation
statement.** That statement is the vision technique's ground, and the boundary is
the reason both sheets exist.

## Invocation contract — check before you run

**BA-invoked, never auto-fired.** This skill starts only from `/ba-run t07`.

Self-check, and stop if either half fails:

> the run is **on the composed plan** of an aspect that is `open` or `reopened` —
> **either** Context, against AT-CX-3, **or** Vision, against AT-VI-2's target
> side; **both entries are legal** — **with its output contract pinned**:
> `{competitive analysis — one entry per named alternative, the status quo screened · Context · .specify/memory/competitive-analysis.md}`.

On a miss, stop and name `/ba-run`, or `/ba-aspect` to open and compose. Under
the Vision plan this is a re-composition: the vision run surfaces the missing
target and the BA composes this technique in ahead of it.

**Say which hole you are running against.** The two entries are not
interchangeable in the report: under Context the run answers "the Unlike section
is empty"; under Vision it answers "the differentiation has no named target".
Both write the same file; they close different criteria.

**Skip-if — refuse the run and say which:** the canvas `Competition.Unlike`
section is filled with entries this artifact backs, **or** an explicit
`N/A — <reason>` ruling stands — **and** no target-side hole remains under
Vision. Deeper teardowns beyond positioning grade are **enrichment on BA ask**.

## Depth boundary — positioning grade, and it is a hard edge

Elicit **per entry**: alternative · category · what it covers · where it falls
short **against a named `P-n` / `O-n`** · source.

**Must NOT descend into:**

- feature-matrix exhaustiveness, pricing teardowns, UX teardowns — enrichment, on
  BA ask only
- **the Our Solution statement.** Targets and deltas travel to the vision run;
  the sentence is written there.
- market sizing or business-case math — presale and value ground
- constraint rows — a competitor-imposed bind, an exclusivity clause, routes to
  `constraints.md`

**A delta that names no problem or objective is decoration, not differentiation
ground.** If the `→ P-n / O-n` key cannot be written, the delta does not go in
the column.

## Inputs loaded

In this order:

1. `canvas.md` — **`Competition.Unlike`, Problems and Objectives first**; the
   Unlike seeds are candidates, the `P-n` / `O-n` lines are the keys
2. presale material and any transcripts on hand
3. `.specify/memory/context.md`

## Procedure

1. **BA act.** The run is invoked under the Context plan against AT-CX-3, or
   under the Vision plan against AT-VI-2's target side.

2. **Framework act — the sweep.** Gather candidates from the presale material,
   the canvas Unlike seeds, and stakeholder mentions in transcripts. **The status
   quo is always screened as an alternative** — the strongest competitor is often
   the current way of working, and it is the one nobody names unprompted.

3. **Framework act — pre-draft the entries.** Every line cited or marked. Deltas
   are written **only where a `P-n` / `O-n` link is real**; a delta that names no
   problem or objective is left out rather than dressed up.

4. **BA act — rule the set.** The BA rules which alternatives matter at this
   grade, confirms or edits the deltas, and rules `N/A — <reason>` where a market
   genuinely does not exist. **The reason is the ruling** — an internal tool with
   no market is a legal end state, an unexplained `N/A` is not.

5. **Framework act — the canvas batch and cross-routing.** The Unlike summary —
   names, plus URLs where known — is proposed to the canvas as a batch. **The
   canvas holds the names; this artifact holds the analysis.** Cross-routing: a
   competitor-imposed bind is proposed to `constraints.md`. A finding
   contradicting **cleared** ground is a **reopen signal**; within still-open
   Context or Vision content it is ordinary correction.

6. **Framework act — write and report.** Write `competitive-analysis.md`. Report
   which criteria the run moved: AT-CX-3 under Context; under Vision, report that
   **AT-VI-2's named-target precondition is satisfied** and that **the criterion
   itself is not met until the statement exists** — that is the vision run's act,
   not this one's. The evidence-table refresh and the confirmation proposal belong
   to `/ba-run`'s post-run touchpoint.

## Output

`.specify/memory/competitive-analysis.md` — one table, five columns:

`| Alternative | Category | Covers | Falls short | Source |`

One row per named alternative, **the status quo among them, always**. Every
`Falls short` cell carries at least one `→ P-n` or `→ O-n` key.

Full no-market case: the single line `N/A — <reason>`.

The template and a worked example are in `references/example.md`.

Plus the canvas `Competition.Unlike` summary batch, and any constraint batch the
cross-routing found.

## Signals

- **Routing batch** — the canvas Unlike summary; competitor-imposed binds toward
  `constraints.md`. Proposed, BA-approved, then written.
- **Reopen signal** — only once the contradicted aspect has been cleared or
  waived: finding · contradicted artifact + line · conflict statement. Emit it and
  stop; the reopen skill receives and rules it.

## What this skill never does

Never writes the differentiation statement · never records a delta with no
`→ P-n / O-n` key · never omits the status quo from the screen · never rules
`N/A` without a reason, or on the BA's behalf · never builds a feature matrix or
a pricing teardown unasked · never writes a constraint row · never edits
`canvas.md` outside an approved batch · never claims AT-VI-2 met — it supplies
that criterion's targets and says so · never confirms an AT criterion or clears
an aspect · never runs a CC assertion.
