# `/ba-t09` — vision & differentiation

**Serves:** Vision. **Class:** Context — the canvas's own class ·
**Destination:** `canvas.md` — `Product.The`, `Product.Is`, `Product.That` and
`Competition.Our Solution`. **No new artifact is born here.**

The vision at **statement grade**: the three product slots, the differentiation
against **named** Unlike entries leaning on their keyed deltas, and then the
**constraint-contradiction scan** — every Confirmed constraint row read once
against the statement, every hit resolved or named before the aspect can clear.
The decision this run lets the BA make: **one product statement — complete,
differentiated against something named, and not at war with what binds.**

## Invocation contract — check before you run

**BA-invoked, never auto-fired.** This skill starts only from `/ba-run t09`.

Self-check, and stop if either half fails:

> the run is **on the composed plan** of the Vision aspect, which is `open` or
> `reopened`, **with its output contract pinned**:
> `{product statement + differentiation against a named Unlike entry, the constraint scan run · Context · canvas.md — Product.The/Is/That · Competition.Our Solution}`.

On a miss, stop and name `/ba-run`, or `/ba-aspect vision` to open and compose.
**Vision is the only two-edge aspect:** it opens on Context **and** Value both
`first-pass-cleared` or waived. If either edge is unmet, say which and stop.

**Skip-if — refuse the run and say which:** AT-VI-1 through AT-VI-3 all read met
in the current evidence table — a complete, differentiated statement carried from
presale, with the scan clean against the current Confirmed set. **Target-side work
is never this run's ground**: entries and deltas belong to the competitive
technique, which is re-invocable under this same Vision plan.

## Depth boundary — statement grade, and it is a hard edge

Elicit **three slots, one line each**, and **one differentiation statement**
naming its targets and leaning on their keyed deltas.

**Must NOT descend into:**

- authoring competitive entries or deltas — the competitive technique's ground
- **enumerating the surface** — forms, functions, connections. That is the
  solution run's ground.
- rewriting value lines. Problems and Objectives are cleared ground by now;
  changes to them **route as proposals**, they are not edited from here.
- **technology or architecture choices dressed as vision.** The *how* is the
  coding agent's, downstream. A vision slot naming a stack has crossed the line.
- brand or marketing copy beyond the three slots — no decision content

## Inputs loaded

In this order:

1. `canvas.md` — the Product slots, `Competition.Our Solution`, and the Problems
   and Objectives lines the differentiation will key to
2. `.specify/memory/competitive-analysis.md` — **entries and keyed deltas; read,
   never authored here**
3. `.specify/memory/constraints.md` — **the Confirmed rows.** They are the scan's
   whole input.
4. `.specify/memory/glossary.md`

## Procedure

1. **BA act.** Vision is opened — both edges met — and its plan composed; the run
   is invoked.

2. **Framework act — pre-draft the statement.** `The` / `Is` / `That` from the
   framing seeds, the presale material and the kickoff notes. `Our Solution`
   drafted against the competitive table's entries and their keyed deltas. **Every
   line cited or marked.**

3. **Framework act — the scan.** **Every `Confirmed` row of `constraints.md` is
   read once against the drafted statement. `Assumed` rows are excluded** — the
   scan tests the statement against what binds, and an assumption does not yet
   bind.

   Per hit, propose a resolution:
   - **statement revision** — the normal case
   - **or**, where the claim may stand, a **conflict marker** on the affected
     statement line, and the constraint challenged as a **reopen signal** on
     Context

   The marker form is fixed:

   > `[CONFLICT: vision claims <X> · constraints.md §<n> "<row>" binds <Y>]`

   **Report the scan as a table — every Confirmed row, and its verdict — even
   when every verdict is "compatible".** A scan that reports only its hits is
   indistinguishable from a scan that did not run, and the criterion reads
   whether the scan ran against the *current* pair.

4. **Framework act — the remaining holes become questions.** Each question is
   **destination-tagged before it is asked**: an empty slot, or a differentiation
   naming no entry. **A question serving neither is illegal and must not be
   emitted.**

   **A missing target is never authored here.** Zero Unlike entries, or entries
   without deltas, is the competitive technique's hole. Surface it as a
   **plan-composition prompt** — add that technique under this same Vision plan —
   and proceed on what stands.

5. **BA act — the rulings.** The BA rules the slots, the statement, and each scan
   hit: **revise** · **challenge** (→ reopen signal on Context) · **name and
   carry** under the marker. On a missing target, the BA re-composes the plan to
   run the competitive technique first.

6. **Framework act — write and report.** Write the four sections under the
   contract. Report which criteria the run moved — AT-VI-1 · AT-VI-2 · AT-VI-3 —
   and what remains open.

   **Clearing over a named conflict is legal** — the criterion's own text allows
   "resolved **or** named as an open conflict". The marker stays visible on the
   canvas and travels into the clearing's evidence line. Say so rather than
   implying the conflict must be resolved first.

   The evidence-table refresh and the confirmation proposal belong to `/ba-run`'s
   post-run touchpoint; the clearing itself is the BA's, at `/ba-clear`.

## Output

`canvas.md`, four sections, at statement grade:

- **`Product.The`** — name or working title
- **`Product.Is`** — solution type, one line
- **`Product.That`** — what it enables for the customer, one line
- **`Competition.Our Solution`** — differentiation against **≥ 1 named Unlike
  entry**, keyed `→ P-n / O-n`; conflict markers where a scan hit is held

**Nothing else in the canvas is touched by this run.**

The template, a worked example and a worked scan are in `references/example.md`.

Plus the reopen signals where a constraint challenge stands.

## Signals

- **Plan-composition prompt** — a missing target: the competitive technique,
  proposed into this same Vision plan. Not a question, not a write.
- **Reopen signal** — a challenged constraint. Context is cleared ground by the
  time Vision opens, so the challenge is always a signal, never a correction:
  finding · contradicted artifact + line · conflict statement. Emit it and stop;
  the reopen skill receives and rules it. The marker stands until the reopen
  resolves.

## What this skill never does

Never authors a competitive entry or delta · never enumerates the solution
surface · never edits Problems or Objectives · never names a technology, a
platform or an architecture in a vision slot · never differentiates against an
unnamed field · never resolves a scan hit on the BA's behalf · never reads
`Assumed` rows into the scan · never reports a scan without its full row table ·
never touches a canvas section it does not own · never confirms an AT criterion
or clears an aspect · never runs a CC assertion.
